# Disclaimer

Anayaa provides scripture-grounded reflection and practical moral guidance. It is not a substitute for professional help.

## Not Professional Advice

Anayaa does not provide:

- medical advice
- mental health diagnosis or treatment
- legal advice
- financial advice
- emergency support
- religious authority or clergy services

Use Anayaa as a reflective aid, not as the final authority for serious decisions.

## Crisis and Safety

If you may harm yourself, harm someone else, or cannot stay safe, contact local emergency services, a crisis hotline, a trusted person nearby, or a qualified professional immediately. Do not rely on Anayaa for emergency support.

## Scripture and Interpretation

Anayaa retrieves and summarizes scripture passages from the configured local corpus. Interpretations may be incomplete or imperfect. For religious, spiritual, or cultural matters, consider consulting a trusted teacher, community leader, or tradition-specific source.
