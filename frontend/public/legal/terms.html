Anayaa.AI Terms of Use

Anayaa provides scripture-grounded reflection and practical moral guidance. Use it as a reflective aid, not as the final authority for serious decisions.

Public Beta

Anayaa is a technical public-beta candidate. Features, local setup requirements, generated guidance, and retention behavior may change as the project evolves.

No Professional Advice

Anayaa does not provide medical advice, mental health diagnosis or treatment, legal advice, financial advice, emergency support, or clergy services. For serious situations, contact a qualified professional, trusted person, community leader, or emergency service as appropriate.

Crisis and Safety

If you may harm yourself, harm someone else, or cannot stay safe, contact local emergency services, a crisis hotline, a trusted person nearby, or a qualified professional immediately. Do not rely on Anayaa for emergency support.

Scripture and Interpretation

Anayaa retrieves and summarizes passages from the configured local corpus. Interpretations may be incomplete or imperfect. For religious, spiritual, or cultural matters, consider consulting a trusted teacher, community leader, or tradition-specific source.

Responsible Use

  • Do not use Anayaa to seek instructions for harm, manipulation, illegal activity, or evading safety systems.
  • Do not enter highly sensitive personal information unless you understand the local storage and privacy behavior.
  • Review retrieved citations and use your own judgment before acting on guidance.

Privacy

Your use is also governed by the Privacy Notice.
